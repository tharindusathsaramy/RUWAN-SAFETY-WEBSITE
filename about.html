About Ruwan Safety

Building trust through quality and safety since 2014.

Our Story

Ruwan Safety began with a simple mission: to provide the Sri Lankan construction industry with reliable, high-quality safety equipment that doesn't compromise on affordability. What started as a small hardware shop has now grown into a premier supplier trusted by major construction firms across the island.

We understand that in our line of work, quality equipment isn't just a requirement—it's a matter of life and safety. That's why we meticulously source our products from certified global manufacturers.
